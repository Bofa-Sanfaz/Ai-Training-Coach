Section 1What changed in 3.3

Ride Partner became Training Partner: one app for riding, running, swimming and gym work. Everything below was changed in this version.

All your sportsRuns from Strava; pool swims, treadmill runs, the gym bike, elliptical, stair stepper, rowing machine and strength sessions straight from the Galaxy Watch through Samsung Health. Each sport has its own colour and icon (RideRunSwimGym & other), its own numbering (Ride 043, Run 002, Swim 001, Gym 001) and its own units (km/h for rides, pace per km for runs, pace per 100 m for swims).
New activity inboxTypes the app doesn't import by default — walks, hikes, yoga, football… — wait in an inbox on the Training screen. Include them (walks and hikes count as runs — since 3.5 as Walks — everything else as Gym & other) or skip them, and optionally remember the choice for that type.
Only 2026 onwardsStrava and Samsung Health imports ignore anything before 1 January 2026.
Mark wrong dataThe flag on every workout (and on every record) opens a sheet: change the sport, mark it indoor, mark climbing, distance/speed, heart rate or power as a data error, exclude it, or delete it. Original numbers are kept; flagged values are simply left out of records, best efforts, trends, totals and AI context.
Delete workoutsDeleting creates a restore point first and remembers the workout so sync never brings it back.
Charts fixedCharts no longer disappear after changing a filter, a metric pill, or coming back to a screen. Charts can't trap your scroll any more, and chart-heavy screens draw after the screen transition so the tab never feels frozen.
Compare really comparesSame-sport sessions, "latest vs most similar" by default (accidental recordings skipped), a one-line verdict, efficiency (speed per heartbeat), paired bars, heart-rate zones side by side and a speed/HR overlay chart.
Model per coach, two-level pickerTap the model line in any coach → a provider (e.g. Google Gemini) → its models. Choose "Use another provider" (automatic fallback) or "Keep retrying this one" (only that model, retried with growing pauses until it answers). Every reply shows the model that wrote it and how long it took.
Messages are never lostYour message is saved the instant you send it. Leaving the chat or the app doesn't stop the reply; unanswered messages are retried automatically when the app comes back or the connection returns.
Coach memoryFacts and corrections ("that ride was a stationary gym bike") are saved and given to every coach. Coaches add facts themselves when you correct them; you can add or delete them in Profile → Coach memory.
Better AI answersReply rules from a review of your real conversations: answer first, ~150 words for chat, no hype, no repeated closing questions, weight only when relevant, no invented numbers or sessions, honest about missing data. "No bike this week" now means other sports, not rest. Timelines show each minute's peak values, not only averages.
More complete, more compact contextAether sees every workout: the last six weeks one by one (with your note and the coach's verdict from that session's chat), older ones as weekly/monthly totals, plus a "data completeness" line. A bug that gave every Gemini model the reduced "small model" context ("gemini") is fixed.
Samsung Health improvementsResting heart rate is estimated from the watch's heart rate (Samsung doesn't share a resting value), the coaches are told when sleep wasn't recorded instead of treating it as bad sleep, and HRV is reported as unavailable.
Reports & PDFChoose All / Rides / Runs / Swims / Gym. The PDF now has a cover band, coach verdict, KPI tiles vs the previous period, each sport, fitness/fatigue and weekly-volume charts, an intensity donut, a week-by-week table, a page per sport (trend chart, zones, top sessions), highlights & records, plan adherence, a recovery log, data-quality notes, coach memory, the review and every session.
Every sport its own numbers (3.4)Swims: SWOLF, strokes per length, stroke rate, stroke mix, rest, best 50–400 m, per-length chart and 100 m splits. Runs: cadence, steps, VO2max. Gym: time, heart rate, load, calories, reps. Feed, detail, Trends, records, Compare, Reports, PDF and the AI coaches use only what fits each sport.
MotionScreens slide in the direction you move, a tapped workout grows into its detail page, numbers roll up, routes draw themselves, a light sweep crosses the week card, the tab indicator slides, buttons ripple, and new records get confetti. All of it is switched off if the phone asks for reduced motion.

Section 2The seven screens

Training · Trends · Compare · Coach · Squad (section 12) · Reports · Profile.

Training

  • Sync (or pull down): Strava plus the watch (Samsung Health workouts and wellness).
  • Inbox card appears when new activity types are waiting (see section 4).
  • Week card: sessions, ride km, time and climbing this week vs last week, progress bars against your weekly targets, streak, form, and a chip per sport (sessions · time).
  • Readiness strip (Samsung Health): last night's sleep, resting HR vs 30-day baseline, HRV when available.
  • Today's planned session, tap to open the plan.
  • Sport chips (All · Rides · Runs · Walks · Swims · Gym & other), shown when you have more than one sport. The choice also applies to Trends.
  • Search across name, date, sport, zone and your notes; filters Training / Play / No intent / Needs review.
  • Workout cards grouped by month: distance, time, speed or pace, HR, ascent (or energy for gym), load, and the zone strip. Flagged values are struck through; excluded workouts are faded.

Trends

  • Fitness · Fatigue · Form KPIs and chart (90 days, 6 months, 1 year, all).
  • Calendar coloured by daily load; tap a day to open it.
  • Weekly volume by distance, time, climbing or load — stacked by sport when you train more than one.
  • Best efforts per sport (1, 5, 20, 60 min speed/pace and HR; power only with a power meter), all-time and last 6 weeks.
  • Session-by-session progression for one sport at a time (speed, HR, efficiency, distance, climbing, work, load), scrollable sideways, tap a point to open it.
  • AI season audit with its own model choice.

Compare

  • Pick the sport, then A and B (and optionally C). Presets: Latest vs most similar, Latest vs previous, Latest vs best similar.
  • Verdict: faster/slower at what heart rate, and the efficiency change; it also says whether the two sessions are similar enough to compare fairly.
  • Paired bars per metric with the B-vs-A difference (green = better for B), zones side by side, overlay chart of speed and HR along the distance (or time for swims/gym).
  • Explain the difference asks the Compare coach; its answer is saved for that pair.

Coach

  • Aether, the season coach (section 7), with the model line under its header and quick prompts.
  • Training plan: this/next week, your weekly template, day changes, targets, done/missed ticks, and "Adapt with AI".
  • Session conversations: every workout has its own coach thread.

Reports

  • Period: 7 days, 30 days, 1 year, all time; activities: All or one sport.
  • Sport tiles with change vs the previous period, load/efficiency/intensity panel, zone bars, all-time records per sport (each with a flag to mark it as a data error), the AI period review and the PDF (section 10).

Profile

  • Athlete (name, height, bike), default weekly targets, weight log, training phase, max/resting HR and zones, FTP.
  • Strava connection; AI providers (add free or paid ones, keys, models), automatic fallback, model per coach; coach memory; coach skills (import from GitHub); Samsung Health; AI spending cap.
  • Appearance (light/dark/system), auto-sync, haptics; backups, restore points, Classic rollback; diagnostics.

Section 3Workout detail

Tap any workout. It grows out of its card into a full page.

  • Header: share card image, edit intent, and the flag (sport, indoor, data errors, exclude, delete).
  • Badges: sport, intent, indoor, Galaxy Watch, power meter, and red badges for anything marked as a data error.
  • Route (outdoor GPS only) coloured by heart-rate zone; it draws itself in.
  • Hero numbers in the sport's units; flagged numbers are struck through (tap them to change the flag).
  • How did it feel? Feel, RPE 1–10 and a private note — all read by the coaches.
  • Biometrics: max speed, peak HR (% of max), load, work, climbing VAM, aerobic decoupling, elapsed time, session-RPE load, and NP/IF/TSS for power-meter rides.
  • Heart-rate zones on moving time, vs your previous comparable sessions (same sport and intent), best efforts in this session, and the stream chart (speed, HR, power, elevation, cadence).
  • AI coach: analyse the session, then chat about it (screenshots allowed). The trash icon next to the title resets that thread.

Swims, treadmill & gym: straight from Samsung Health

Samsung Health shares fewer workouts with Health Connect than it records, and no swims at all. Training Partner therefore also reads Samsung Health directly (Samsung Health Data SDK, read-only), on every sync: pool and open-water swims with distance, pool length and active swim time (pace per 100 m), treadmill and outdoor runs with heart rate and speed, the stationary bike, elliptical, rowing machine, stairs and strength sets — each with the watch's second-by-second heart rate, so zones and load are real. Outdoor rides come from Strava and rest breaks between sets are skipped; walks, squash and other types go to the inbox.

One-time setup: Samsung Health → ⋮ → Settings → About Samsung Health → tap the version 10 times → Developer mode (Samsung Health Data SDK) → Developer Mode for Data Read on; then Profile → Samsung Health → Connect and allow Exercise.

Fallback: Samsung Health export

Samsung Health does not share swim sessions with Health Connect (other watch workouts and heart rate do arrive). To bring swims in: Samsung Health → ⋮ → Settings → Download personal data; then in Training Partner, Profile → Samsung Health → Import Samsung Health export and pick com.samsung.shealth.exercise….csv from Download › Samsung Health › newest folder. Also selecting the files in jsons › com.samsung.shealth.exercise adds second-by-second heart rate (zones, load). Sessions already in the app (±5 min) are skipped; walks, hikes and custom types go to the inbox. Repeat the export whenever you want the newest swims.

When Strava files a watch treadmill or exercise-bike session as a generic sleep with stages (counted on the morning you wake up, only asleep stages), steps, weight (optionally copied to the weight log), VO2max, HRV if shared, and resting HR. Samsung Health doesn't share resting HR, so the app estimates it as the day's lowest hourly average heart rate (usually during sleep), marked "estimated". 120 days are kept.

Permissions are read-only: heart rate, resting HR, HRV, sleep, steps, weight, VO2max, SpO2, respiratory rate, exercise sessions, distance, active calories, and history access. The first manual import after this update asks for the new ones once.

The inbox

Each waiting activity shows type → sport, date, distance and duration. Ticked ones are added (walks and hikes as Walks, others as Gym & other), unticked ones are skipped for good. "Every walk from now on" can be set to Include or Skip; you can change it next time the inbox opens.

Section 6Calculations

Heart-rate zones

Five zones from your max HR and four boundaries (default for max 207: 135 / 155 / 171 / 189 bpm). Suggested boundaries are 65.2 %, 74.9 %, 82.6 % and 91.3 % of max. Zone time counts moving time only; gaps longer than 10 s count as 10 s.

Training load — Banister TRIMP

HRr = (HR − resting HR) / (max HR − resting HR), clamped 0…1 TRIMP = Σ (Δt in minutes) × HRr × a × e^(b × HRr) a = 0.64, b = 1.92 (male) · a = 0.86, b = 1.67 (female)

Computed from the HR stream (all recorded time); without a stream, from average HR × moving minutes. Works for every sport, so load can be added across sports.

Fitness, Fatigue, Form

CTL(today) = CTL(yesterday) + (load − CTL(yesterday)) / 42 Fitness ATL(today) = ATL(yesterday) + (load − ATL(yesterday)) / 7 Fatigue TSB(today) = CTL(yesterday) − ATL(yesterday) Form Ramp = CTL now − CTL 7 days ago ACWR = ATL / CTL

Form labels: > +15 very fresh/detraining, +5…+15 fresh, −10…+5 balanced, −30…−10 productive fatigue, < −30 high fatigue.

Speed, pace, efficiency

  • Average speed of a group = total distance ÷ total moving time of the same sessions (duration-weighted), computed only within one sport.
  • Run pace = 60 ÷ km/h (min/km); swim pace = 6 ÷ km/h (min/100 m).
  • Efficiency = average speed ÷ average HR × 100 (km/h per 100 bpm). Higher = more speed for the same cardiac cost. Reports use rides ≥ 20 min, weighted by duration.

Climbing VAM

Metres climbed per hour while moving on grades above 3 % (Strava's smoothed grade, or altitude/distance). Needs at least 2 minutes of climbing and 5 m of gain.

Aerobic decoupling and drift

On moving time, after skipping the first 12 minutes, the rest is split into two halves. Only for steady training sessions (intent Training, not intervals) of 30+ minutes.

EF = speed / HR (power / HR with a power meter) Decoupling % = (EF₁ − EF₂) / EF₁ × 100 Drift = HR₂ − HR₁ (bpm)

Best efforts

Streams are resampled to 1 sample per second; the best rolling average over 1, 5, 20 and 60 minutes is kept for speed and HR (every session) and for power (5 s … 60 min, power meter only).

Power (power meter only)

NP = (mean of (30-s rolling average power)⁴)^¼ FTP = your setting, or 95 % of the best 20-min power of the last 120 days IF = NP / FTP TSS = hours × IF² × 100

Strava's power estimate for rides without a power meter is shown but never used for calculations.

Intensity distribution

Low = Z1+Z2, middle = Z3, high = Z4+Z5 of zone time. Polarization index = (low + high) ÷ middle; above 2 means a polarized distribution.

Swim metrics

The watch logs every pool length: its time, stroke count and stroke style. From that the app computes:

Swim time = Σ length times (rest between sets excluded) Rest = total − swim time Pace /100 m = swim time ÷ distance × 100 SWOLF (one length) = seconds + strokes Avg SWOLF = mean over stroke lengths (kick-board lengths excluded) Strokes per length = strokes ÷ stroke lengths Stroke rate = strokes ÷ stroke-length minutes Best 50/100/200/400 m = fastest run of consecutive lengths covering that distance 100 m splits = consecutive groups of 100 m of lengths

Lower SWOLF, fewer strokes per length and a lower pace mean more efficient swimming. Heart-rate zones and load come from the watch's second-by-second heart rate over the whole session.

Run and gym metrics

Runs add cadence (steps per minute, average and max), total steps and the watch's VO2max estimate; treadmill runs have no GPS climbing. Gym sessions show time, average/peak/lowest heart rate, load, calories and repetitions (when the watch counts them) — no distance or speed.

Records

Per sport, ignoring excluded workouts and flagged values: longest distance, longest time, most climbing and best VAM (rides/runs), best average speed or pace (rides ≥ 10 km, runs ≥ 3 km, swims ≥ 400 m), top speed (rides), hardest session (TRIMP), biggest week. New records after a sync trigger a toast and confetti.

Compare similarity

score = 1.2 × |Δdistance| + |Δduration| + 0.4 × |Δclimbing| (each relative to the larger) + 0.6 if the intent differs + 0.5 if one is indoor and the other isn't

Below 0.5 = very similar, below 1 = reasonably similar. "Real" sessions are at least 5 minutes and 0.5 km (0.1 km swims).

Timeline given to the ride coach

Per minute (2 minutes for small models) of moving time: distance, average and peak speed (or best pace), average and peak HR, average and peak power, elevation and climb/descent.

Style rules (all coaches)

  • Answer in the first sentence; chat replies ~150 words unless you ask for detail; debriefs/comparisons ~300 words; reviews thorough but tight.
  • Calm and factual, no hype or emojis; body weight only when it matters; no closing question unless needed and never repeated.
  • Only numbers from the context; never invent sessions, symptoms or events; say when data is missing or flagged; readiness without sleep/HRV/resting HR is labelled "load only".
  • Lasting facts and corrections are saved with <remember> into the coach memory.

Section 8Models, retries and costs

  • Providers: Google Gemini, Groq, OpenRouter, Mistral (free tiers) and Anthropic Claude, OpenAI, DeepSeek (paid), or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint. Keys stay on the phone.
  • Picker: tap the model line in a coach → Automatic or a provider → its models (searchable; free/price tags; "Refresh" reloads the list). Apply to this coach or all coaches. Pick chat models — code-only models answer poorly.
  • Automatic / Use another provider: the chosen model first, then your default, then the others (free ones first). A toast says who answered.
  • Keep retrying this one: only that model; on rate limits, busy servers, timeouts or no connection it waits 10 s, 20 s, 40 s, 1, 2, 3, then 5 min between tries, for up to 2 hours, showing the countdown under the typing indicator. A wrong key, unknown model or bad request stops at once.
  • Timeouts: 75 s for the first streamed word, 45 s silence while streaming, 150 s without streaming.
  • Spending: paid providers count tokens × your prices into a monthly total; at 80 % of the cap you're warned, at 100 % paid providers stop (free ones keep working).
  • Never lost: Aether and session chats save your message first (marked "sending"), finish in the background, notify you if you left, and retry unanswered messages (last 3 days) when the app returns or the connection comes back.

Section 9Plan, targets and Aether's changes

  • Weekly template: one session per weekday that repeats. Day changes override a single date (yours, the AI planner's or Aether's — each marked). A session can carry a sport.
  • Done = a workout that day or your tick; past planned days without either show as missed.
  • Weekly targets: km (riding), hours (all sports) and sessions — default, or for this/next week only.
  • Aether's changes arrive as a card with Apply / Dismiss. Apply creates a restore point first. Aether proposes changes only when you ask or agree; "no bike" means other sports unless you say rest, injury or illness.
  • Adapt with AI (planner) rewrites the next 7 days around your template, form, wellness and missed sessions.

Section 10Reports and the PDF

Pick the period and the activities, write the review (optional), then Export PDF; it opens the share sheet. Pages:

  1. Cover band, coach verdict, six KPI tiles vs the previous period, per-sport breakdown with time share, Fitness/Fatigue/Form chart.
  2. Weekly training time stacked by sport, intensity donut and zone bars, recovery snapshot and best efforts, week-by-week table (sessions per sport, time, km/m, TRIMP, fitness/form, vs target).
  3. One page per sport: summary line, speed/pace vs heart-rate chart, zone split, top sessions table.
  4. Highlights and records set in the period, plan adherence and missed sessions, recovery log (sleep, stages, resting HR, HRV, steps, weight), data-quality notes and coach memory.
  5. The AI review with numbered sections, then every session colour-coded by sport.

Section 11Backups, privacy and updates

  • Everything lives on the phone (IndexedDB). Backups and restore points never include API keys or Strava tokens.
  • Restore points are made automatically before syncs, updates, applying plan changes, restoring a backup and deleting a workout; up to six automatic and six manual are kept.
  • Updates install over the old version (same signing key), so data stays. Classic (v2) interface remains available as a rollback.
  • USB debugging of the app's WebView is enabled for diagnostics; it only works from a computer you've authorised for USB debugging.

Section 12 · new in 3.5Squad — training together

The Squad tab is a small app of its own for challenges with friends (made for training with Assad: iPhone, Huawei Watch Fit 5 Pro, mostly walking and swimming). Your personal side stays exactly as it was — the squad only receives summaries. Walks are now their own sport too (Walk: pace /km, steps, cadence) instead of counting as runs.

Fair scoring — everyone against himself

Different bodies, sports and starting points never decide the winner. Each person's baseline comes from his own 4 weeks before the challenge (or his first challenge week).

weekly points = consistency 40 × min(active days / own target, 1) + volume 30 × min(minutes / own usual, 1.5) / 1.5 + improvement 30 × (0.5 + clamp(pace better than own usual / 5 %, −1, 1) / 2) + bonuses: PB +5 each (max 15) · streak day of 3+ in a row +1 (max 7) · over target +3

Improvement uses swim pace /100 m, walk and run pace /km and ride speed, weighted by minutes per sport. The higher relative score wins the week (weekly duel); the month goes to the higher average weekly points. Weight is never compared: graphs, the PDF and Aether only ever see each person's change.

What is shared

  • Automatically after every sync, for workouts from the challenge start: sport, date and time, duration, distance, pace/speed, heart rate, load, SWOLF and every swim length, cadence, steps, climbing, personal bests (vs your own earlier workouts of that sport, after 3 of them).
  • Unless switched off: how it felt (RPE), your note and the first sentence of your coach's debrief.
  • Only if switched on (opt-in): weight, resting HR, HRV, sleep and daily steps. Switching it off removes them from the squad.
  • Editing or deleting a workout updates or removes it in the squad too.

Aether in the squad

One strong model with the challenge owner's key, kept on the squad server (friends never see the key; the monthly spend cap applies). It answers @Aether, Review, Roast and the monthly report, and it can chime in on its own — personal bests, streaks, a first workout, someone quiet for 3 days, or a long chat without it — but never within 90 seconds of someone writing, at most once per 2 hours and 4 times a day, and it may stay silent. A daily recap arrives at the chosen hour (the last day of each week also announces the duel), and a final recap when the challenge ends. Only one AI reply is written at a time; replies are attached to the message they answer. Roasts are about the workout, never about bodies, weight, health or anything personal.

Sync — never stops working

The squad server is a Cloudflare Worker with a D1 database on the free plan: serverless, nothing pauses after hours or days without use. Your posts go into an outbox first, so closing the app or losing signal never loses them, and a retry can never post twice. The app checks for news every 4 s while the chat is open, every 15 s on the Squad tab, every minute elsewhere and immediately when the app opens.
