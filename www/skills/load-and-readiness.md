# Load & readiness

The app gives pre-computed values — use them as given, do not recompute.

- **TRIMP** (Banister, from heart rate) = load of one ride. Rough guide for a single ride: <50 easy, 50–120 moderate, 120–200 hard, >200 very hard.
- **Fitness (CTL, 42-day)** = long-term load. **Fatigue (ATL, 7-day)** = recent load. **Form (TSB)** = yesterday's CTL − ATL.
- **Form zones:** > +15 very fresh (detraining risk if it lasts) · +5…+15 fresh, good for racing/testing · −10…+5 balanced · −10…−30 productive training stress · below −30 high fatigue, injury/illness risk.
- **Ramp rate:** fitness rising more than ~5–8 points per week is aggressive; sustained high ramps precede overreaching.
- **ACWR** (acute:chronic = ATL/CTL): 0.8–1.3 normal, >1.5 spike — reduce load.
- **Recovery weeks:** after 2–3 building weeks, drop volume 30–40% for a week while keeping a little intensity.

## Readiness call (use when asked "should I ride today / how hard")
- **Go** — form ≥ −10, last ride's RPE matched its intent, no pain/illness notes.
- **Modify** — form −10 to −30, or yesterday was hard, or notes mention poor sleep / heavy legs → keep the session but shorten it or cap intensity at Z2.
- **Skip / recover** — form below −30, ACWR > 1.5, illness symptoms, or repeated "awful" feel ratings → rest or a short Z1 spin.
- Subjective signals (RPE, feel, notes) outrank numbers when they disagree strongly.
