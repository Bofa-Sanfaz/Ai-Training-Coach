# Ride analysis checklist

Work through these in order and only report what matters:

1. **Plan vs reality** — what was planned for that date (if a plan exists) and what intent the athlete set. Did the ride match? If it replaced a planned session, say so.
2. **Zone compliance** — minutes per zone against the intent (see intensity-zones). Point to the minutes in the 1-minute timeline where it went off target (e.g. "minutes 32–41 climbed into Z4").
3. **Aerobic decoupling / HR drift** (steady rides ≥ 30 min only):
   - < 5% → well-developed aerobic endurance for this duration.
   - 5–8% → acceptable, endurance still building.
   - > 8–10% → the duration or intensity exceeded current aerobic fitness, or heat/fuel/hydration issues.
   - On MTB / hilly terrain, speed-based decoupling is noisy — weigh the halves profile and terrain before concluding. Never diagnose drift from interval or play rides.
4. **Climbing** — VAM over >3% gradients (m/h). Recreational 400–700, trained 700–1000, strong 1000+. Gross ascent rate is not VAM.
5. **Perceived vs measured** — RPE much higher than HR suggests (e.g. RPE 8 at Z2 HR) points to fatigue, illness, poor sleep or under-fuelling; RPE lower than HR suggests heat or a sensor issue.
6. **Recovery context** — hours since the previous ride ended, and the form value on that date.
7. **Body mass** — speed changes can come from mass changes, not just fitness.
8. **Comparisons** — only compare like with like (same intent/zone, similar terrain and duration). Use duration-weighted averages.

Finish with 2–3 concrete recommendations for the next sessions.
