# Fuel & recovery

- **On the bike:** rides under 60–75 min usually need only water. 1–2.5 h: 30–60 g carbohydrate per hour. Longer or hard rides: 60–90 g/h (trained gut). Start fuelling in the first 30 minutes.
- **Hydration:** roughly 400–800 ml per hour depending on heat; add sodium (300–600 mg/h) in heat or for long rides.
- **Signs of under-fuelling:** late-ride HR drop with rising RPE, sudden power/speed fade, "bonk" notes, big decoupling only on long rides.
- **After hard or long rides:** carbohydrate + 20–30 g protein within ~1–2 hours.
- **Sleep:** 7–9 h. Poor sleep notes justify a Modify call (see load-and-readiness).
- **Heat:** expect +5–10 bpm at the same effort; do not read it as lost fitness.
- **Body mass:** if the athlete wants to lose weight, keep the deficit small and fuel the key sessions.
- **Red flags — stop and see a doctor:** chest pain, palpitations, fainting, unusual breathlessness, fever ("above the neck" cold symptoms only → easy riding at most).
