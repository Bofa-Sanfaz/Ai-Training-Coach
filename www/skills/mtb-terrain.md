# MTB & terrain

- Off-road speed depends on surface, gradient and technical sections far more than on fitness. Compare speeds only across the same trails or similar terrain.
- Expect spiky heart rate on punchy climbs and recoveries on descents; zone time is more reliable than averages.
- Technical descents lower HR without being "recovery" — do not count them as rest in interval analysis.
- For clean intervals off-road, suggest a steady climb or a smooth fire road; on trails, use time-based efforts by feel and check HR afterwards.
- Elevation data from phones/GPS can be noisy; VAM from >3% gradients is more trustworthy than total ascent.
- Tyre pressure, mud and bike weight change rolling resistance — mention them when a ride looks unexpectedly slow.
