Ride Partner — privacy

Applies to the Ride Partner Android app.

What the app reads

  • Strava: your rides and their streams, after you sign in to Strava.
  • Health Connect (only if you link it): sleep, resting heart rate, heart-rate variability, heart rate, weight, steps, VO2max, oxygen saturation and respiratory rate — read-only. Ride Partner never writes health data.

Where your data is kept

Everything is stored only on this phone, in the app's private storage. There is no Ride Partner server and no account. Backups you export are files you control.

When data leaves the phone

Only when you ask an AI coach something. The question and the training and wellness data needed to answer it are sent to the AI provider you chose in Profile (for example Google Gemini, Groq, OpenRouter, Mistral, Anthropic, OpenAI or DeepSeek), using your own API key. Their privacy terms apply to that request. Nothing is sent to any other party.

Your control

  • Revoke Health Connect access any time in Android Settings → Health Connect.
  • Pause the import in Profile → Samsung Health.
  • Uninstalling the app deletes all of its data from the phone.
